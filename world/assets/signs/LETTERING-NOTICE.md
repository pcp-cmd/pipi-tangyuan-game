The 16 Chinese characters painted into these SVG illustrations were rasterized
at 17, 22 or 24 pixels from the installed Noto Sans CJK SC Bold (collection index
2), then converted to horizontal pixel-run paths with threshold 128.
Source font: Noto CJK, https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk (SIL OFL 1.1).
The installed package copyright and license are retained in NOTO-LICENSE.txt.
No font file is embedded or required to play. The plaques are static illustrations,
not a redistributable font. Rebuild helper: tools/build-sign-lettering.py.
